## EditNotes

Drops timecode markers in the EditNotes Mac app over OSC.

Set **Target IP** to the Mac running EditNotes (127.0.0.1 if Companion is on the same Mac) and **Port** to the
UDP port shown in EditNotes > Settings > OSC (9300 by default).

### Actions

- **Trigger shortcut** – add the marker for one of your EditNotes shortcuts, by label (`GFX`) or by its number in the list.
- **Add note** – add a free-text marker, with an optional marker name and colour.
- **Remove last marker** – undo the most recently added marker.
